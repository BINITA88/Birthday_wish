Only three sounds are used (see js/config.js, audio.tracks). Their loudness and fades are baked into the files,
because an iPhone ignores any volume set by the page - every phone now plays them exactly the same.
  evergreen.m4a       the song of the whole story, from Begin to the final wish
                      (7 minutes of the "Evergreen Hindi Songs" guitar medley, starting at 9:13 of the original)
  candle.m4a          the candle sound: the cake's sparkler (6.5 s, fades out by itself)
  happy-birthday.m4a  the last tune: arrives with the wish, balloons and confetti
